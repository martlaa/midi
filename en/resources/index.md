## Academic publishing

### Informatics in Education

In June 2026, Tallinn University and Vilnius University assumed joint management of *Informatics in Education*, an open-access research journal on informatics education.

[Journal website](https://infedu.vu.lt/journal/INFEDU)

## Submitted manuscripts

Between June and September 2026, two manuscripts were submitted to CERME 2027:

- A manuscript on the LAHEMATE pre-pilot.
- A manuscript on CTandME, prepared with researchers at NTNU, Vilnius University and Uppsala University.

These are descriptions of the topics, not official article titles. Submission does not imply acceptance or publication.

To find members’ published work, search by name in [ETIS](https://www.etis.ee/).
