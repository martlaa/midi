## Teachers as researchers

MIDIx is based on involving teachers from the early stages of a study. Teachers can contribute to defining the research problem, trying teaching methods and interpreting results. The network draws inspiration from ÜPUI, MMM and Tallinn University’s EduLabs.

<div class="callout"><p>A shared research problem and study design leave room for an individual teacher or a pair of teachers to explore an additional question of their own.</p></div>

## Research designed together

The collaboration model described in MIDIx seminars uses shared core instruments and data collection in pairs, for example through classroom observation and teaching analytics. The March 2025 MIDI meeting discussed launching the network and involving teachers in the LAHEMATE pilot.

## LAHEMATE

LAHEMATE, a design research study involving mathematics educators at Tallinn University and the University of Tartu, started in March 2025. Its summer school in Tartu on 29–30 June 2026 brought together 50 mathematics teachers to work on teaching problem solving.

[Explore the LAHEMATE project]({{ '/en/projects/#lahemate' | relative_url }})

## Seminar topics

- Flexible learning paths and the distinction between mathematical task complexity and difficulty.
- The Thinking Classroom approach and its adaptation to Estonian schools.
- Involving teachers in study design and data collection.
- Sharing teaching methods and supporting collaboration between teachers.
