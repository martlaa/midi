## Our purpose

MIDI advances and shares research and development in mathematics and informatics education. We connect the planning of studies and projects with teacher education, doctoral development and collaboration in Estonia and internationally.

The group’s agenda includes supporting master’s and doctoral research, organising research conferences and teacher summer schools, publishing research and developing a digital knowledge base for the field.

## One community, different ways of working together

- **MIDI** is the mathematics and informatics didactics research group.
- **miniMIDI** is the doctoral seminar supporting research and the next generation of researchers.
- **MIDIx** is MIDI’s teacher-researcher network, connecting research with teaching practice.

## Since 2023

MIDI held its first meeting on 5 September 2023. The initial agenda included regular meetings, a doctoral seminar and open seminars.

## Collaboration

Activities in 2026 include teacher summer schools with the University of Tartu and TalTech, and joint management of *Informatics in Education* with Vilnius University. CTandME involves collaboration with researchers at NTNU, Vilnius University and Uppsala University.

International activities include joint seminars with the University of Turku’s TRILA institute and visits to the mathematics departments at Tampere University and the University of Helsinki. MIDI organised ISSEP 2026 and is preparing NORMA 2027.
